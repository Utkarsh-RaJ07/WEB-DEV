About Our Department

The Department of Biotechnology encourages students to explore biological sciences through laboratory experiments, research activities, and scientific learning.

Our academic activities focus on molecular biology, genetics, microbiology, bioinformatics, and biotechnology research.

Laboratory Safety Guidelines

  • Wear appropriate personal protective equipment.
  • Follow the instructions provided by laboratory supervisors.
  • Never eat or drink inside the laboratory.
  • Handle biological samples according to laboratory protocols.
  • Report spills, accidents, and unsafe conditions immediately.
